# Teaching review

A source review sampled B1 housing/relative clauses (`b1-u1-l1`,
`b1-u1-l3`, `b1-level-checkpoint`) and B2 formal writing/register
(`b2-u4-l2`, `b2-u4-l3`, `b2-u5-l1`), alongside the shared `sein`
explanation and the Bulgarian bakery conversation (`SCENARIO_SCRIPTS[0]`).
Both English and Bulgarian explanations were compared with the German forms.

Corrections made on 2 October 2026:

- The Bulgarian `sein` comparison described omission of a **verb**, while its
  examples omitted a **subject** and retained `съм`. It now describes subject
  omission and limits the German rule to ordinary declarative sentences.
- B2 formal writing claimed that German does not use questions for polite
  requests. It now explicitly accepts `Könnten Sie mir bitte … mitteilen?`
  and `… bestätigen?`, alongside `Ich bitte um …`.
- The B2 genitive heading claimed that the case survives only in writing.
  It now describes formal written usage without excluding spoken genitives.
- B1 author guidance overstated transfer from Bulgarian relative pronouns.
  It now distinguishes familiar agreement from German case and verb placement.
- The provisional 20-second session estimate no longer claims an unsupported
  course-wide measurement.

The existing content suite checks every accepted answer, path-scoped text,
IDs/references, exercise structures and mastery/checkpoint coverage. The new
catalog checks also compare startup metadata with all authored lesson counts,
step IDs, section IDs, review targeting and category-practice counts, then
verify that a cold lesson hydrates actual answers in place before use.

These are code and editorial checks, **not a German educator's sign-off**.
No external educator was available during this work. For a qualified review,
use the sample above first, then all advanced writing and scenario scripts:

| Criterion | Review question |
| --- | --- |
| Natural German | Is each accepted sentence idiomatic in the stated setting? Are other natural variants unfairly rejected? |
| Register | Are informal, formal and administrative examples labelled? Does a rule wrongly turn a preference into a prohibition? |
| Translation | Do both teaching paths preserve tense, politeness, meaning and negation? |
| Bulgarian transfer | Does the contrast describe Bulgarian accurately, including subject omission and limited residual case marking? |
| Feedback | Does a trap explain the actual mistake and allow the learner to repair it? |
| Assessment | Does the checkpoint test taught material at a fair level? Are free-writing constraints justified? |
| Local guidance | Are tenancy, health and official-procedure examples framed as language practice, without universal legal guarantees? |

Record the educator, review date, content IDs and any accepted alternatives.
The course labels describe intended teaching progression; automated checks
cannot certify CEFR attainment.
